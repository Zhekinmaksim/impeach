A model can know the answer, or remember it. Impeach puts the claim through a public trial.

  1. 1

    Commit the claim.

    The publisher swears to a score, posts a bond, and commits the hashes of the test items.

  2. 2

    Challenge the score.

    A challenger stakes against the claim. Rewrites must pass two referee framings before they count.

  3. 3

    Record the verdict.

    Validators call the subject and reach consensus. The resulting certificate exposes the evidence.

    Spent items

    Revealing an item makes its text public. This court marks it spent immediately, so later claims cannot reuse it as a sealed item. The registry records both the sealed commitment and the hash of its normalized text.

    Check from a terminal

    python3 cli/impeach.py check --text "…the item…"
python3 cli/impeach.py check <sha256> --address $CONTRACT
python3 cli/impeach.py recompute web/case.json

    The first two exit with status 1 when the item is spent, so they can gate a CI job. The third rebuilds every verdict on this page from the published probes.

    What this does not show

    A gap is evidence, not proof: every model drops a little on rewritten questions, and the threshold is a convention. The raw counts are published so a reader can disagree with it. Only subjects that answer a public endpoint can be tried.
